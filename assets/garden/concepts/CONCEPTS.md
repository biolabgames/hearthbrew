# Five alternative garden layouts

Environment concepts generated with built-in imagegen. All retain the room's
warm painted style, a single continuous growing plot, and weathered green timber.
These are visual alternatives for choosing a direction; they are not switched
into the playable garden.

1. **Cottage courtyard** — a sheltered diagonal plot between cottage wings,
   under a flowering pergola. `01-cottage-courtyard.png`
2. **The herb walk** — a deep, narrow side garden, with the plot running along
   the cottage and a stone path leading to a distant door. `02-long-herb-walk.png`
3. **Sunlit conservatory** — an airy glasshouse, with overhead glazing, brick
   paving and a large planted bed beside the aisle. `03-sunlit-conservatory.png`
4. **Woodland sanctuary** — an open forest clearing, seen from the cottage
   porch, with a broader polygonal plot and a winding path. `04-woodland-sanctuary.png`
5. **Riverside garden** — a terrace overlooking a stream and bridge, with the
   cottage at the edge of the composition. `05-riverside-walled-garden.png`

The growing herbs are painted into these concepts to show their intended
atmosphere and scale. A selected concept would need an empty-soil background
and matching live plant positions for playable growth and harvesting.

Exact generation prompts and original output paths are recorded in
`prompts.json` in this folder.
