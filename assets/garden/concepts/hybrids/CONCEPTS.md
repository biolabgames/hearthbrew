# Garden and conservatory hybrids

Six alternative scene concepts combining the current outdoor garden with the
sunlit conservatory concept. All retain one uninterrupted timber herb plot,
painted herbs, warm countryside illustration and coherent receding perspective.

1. **Cottage lean-to** — a glass roof attached to the cottage, with open front
   and right sides. `01-cottage-lean-to.png`
2. **Open glass pavilion** — a freestanding glazed gable over the herb plot,
   open to the garden on all sides. `02-open-glass-pavilion.png`
3. **Wide-door conservatory** — glass walls and roof framing a broad opening
   to the flowering outdoor garden. `03-wide-door-conservatory.png`
4. **Glass and vine pergola** — half-glazed timber trellis with roses and an
   open view through the remaining roof. `04-glass-and-vine-pergola.png`
5. **Curved glass alcove** — a glazed rear alcove shelters the back of the
   garden while the front remains outdoors. `05-curved-glass-alcove.png`
6. **Sunken glass courtyard** — a sheltered garden below entrance steps,
   surrounded by low stone walls beneath a glass roof. `06-sunken-glass-courtyard.png`

Created with built-in imagegen using `assets/garden/herb-garden.png` and
`assets/garden/concepts/03-sunlit-conservatory.png` as visual references.
Exact prompts and original output paths are in `prompts.json` in this folder.

These are design alternatives. Their painted-in herbs show atmosphere and
layout; a chosen scene would be prepared for live growth and harvesting before
integration into the game.
