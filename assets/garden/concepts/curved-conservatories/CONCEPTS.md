# Sunlit conservatory × curved glass alcove

Six environment concepts combining the airy sunlit conservatory with the curved
glass alcove. Each retains one continuous timber herb plot and the established
warm painted apothecary style.

1. **Classic curved conservatory** — a straight glazed room ending in a rounded
   window apse. `01-classic-apsidal-conservatory.png`
2. **Wraparound glasshouse** — glass surrounds the plot on three sides beneath
   a low domed roof. `02-wraparound-glasshouse.png`
3. **Side-bay conservatory** — a straight cottage wall faces an asymmetrical
   curved glass side bay. `03-side-bay-conservatory.png`
4. **Fan-roof botanical nook** — a compact curved room beneath radiating roof
   beams. `04-fan-roof-botanical-nook.png`
5. **Long winter garden** — a deep narrow glasshouse with repeated roof arches
   leading toward its rounded end. `05-long-wintergarden.png`
6. **Wide garden orangery** — a low spacious room with a shallow curved rear
   wall and a broad transverse herb plot. `06-wide-garden-orangery.png`

Created with built-in imagegen using the sunlit conservatory and curved glass
alcove concepts as references. Exact prompts and original output paths are in
`prompts.json` in this folder. These are scene alternatives for selection, with
painted-in herbs to show the intended atmosphere and planting scale.
